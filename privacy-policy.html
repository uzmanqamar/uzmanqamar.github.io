PlantGo+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlantGo+ ("PlantGo+", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the PlantGo+ mobile application. PlantGo+ provides plant identification, plant health analysis, local care reminders, scan history, and premium subscription features.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the app, PlantGo+ may process the following categories of information:

  • Photos and images you choose to upload or capture for plant identification or plant health analysis.
  • Plant names, reminder types, reminder times, and reminder frequencies when you create care reminders.
  • Plant scan and diagnosis results saved in your local history on your device.
  • Subscription and purchase status information returned by Apple so the app can determine whether premium access is active.
  • Basic technical or diagnostic information generated by Apple or third-party services as part of operating purchases, notifications, and app functionality.

2. Information We Access Through Device Permissions

PlantGo+ may request access to the following on your device:

  • Camera, so you can take photos of plants.
  • Photo Library, so you can choose existing photos.
  • Notifications, so local care reminders can alert you on your device.

You can manage these permissions at any time in your device settings. If you deny a permission, some features may not be available or may work in a limited way.

3. How We Use Information

We use information processed through the app to:

  • identify plants and return plant identification results;
  • analyze plant health issues and generate diagnosis guidance;
  • save local history and reminders on your device;
  • schedule and deliver local reminder notifications;
  • verify premium subscription access and restore purchases through Apple;
  • maintain, improve, and secure the app.

4. Third-Party Processing

When you use plant identification or plant health analysis, selected images and related request data are transmitted from your device to Plant.id to process the request and return results. Plant.id acts as a third-party service provider for those features. Its handling of data is subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

Subscription purchases, billing, introductory offers, renewals, and restorations are processed by Apple through the App Store. PlantGo+ does not receive your full payment card information.

5. Local Storage on Your Device

PlantGo+ stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • saved reminder details;
  • saved scan history and diagnosis history;
  • app onboarding and access state;
  • cached app preferences.

Local reminder information remains on your device unless you delete it in the app or remove the app from your device. Scan and diagnosis history saved by the app also remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

6. Data Retention

PlantGo+ itself does not operate a separate user account backend for storing your reminders or scan history. Most reminder and history data used by the app is stored locally on your device.

Data sent to Apple or Plant.id may be retained by those providers under their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only as needed with:

  • Plant.id, to perform plant identification and health analysis you request;
  • Apple, to process subscriptions, purchases, and related App Store functionality;
  • service providers or authorities when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

8. Tracking and Advertising

PlantGo+ is not designed as an advertising or cross-app tracking product. If our practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy and our App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

9. Children’s Privacy

PlantGo+ is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to provide valid consent for data processing. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the situation.

10. Your Choices

You can:

  • choose whether to grant camera, photo library, and notification permissions;
  • delete reminders and local history from inside the app where available;
  • cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account settings;
  • stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.

11. International Use

If you use PlantGo+ from outside the country where the app publisher is located, your information may be processed in other jurisdictions through Apple, Plant.id, and related service providers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice in the app or on our website.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the contact information provided for PlantGo+ on our website or app listing.
